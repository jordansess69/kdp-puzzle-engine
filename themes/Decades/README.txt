THEMED DECADE WORDSEARCH DATABASE — VERSION 3 / EDITORIALLY REVIEWED

This version is the cleaner commercial-production source set.

INCLUDED
• 1950s, 1960s, 1970s, 1980s, 1990s, 2000s
• General edition: 100 themed puzzles per decade
• Men edition: 50 themed puzzles per decade
• Women edition: 50 themed puzzles per decade
• 12 words/phrases per puzzle
• 1,200 total puzzle banks / 14,400 placements

WHAT CHANGED FROM V2
• Removed duplicate word banks.
• Balanced vocabulary usage so the same terms do not dominate a book.
• Generalized a number of brand/franchise-heavy proper nouns.
• Replaced several politically heavy or awkward gift-book terms with broader
  historical/everyday-life language.
• Preserved decade-specific categories: music, fashion, cars, technology,
  school, toys, entertainment, food, travel, sports, home life and slang.
• Kept multi-word phrases readable in the source files.
